POST 3 — vorelik-en-3-what-to-say-on-the-phone.png  (tip)
You have a list of businesses with no website. Now what do you say? 📞
3 lines that work:
➊ Start with what's going well: "I saw your 120 reviews on Google, but no website for people to find you."
➋ Talk about their customers, not tech: "Your customers look for your hours and prices."
➌ Offer to show, not to quote: "I can show you in 10 minutes what yours could look like."
To practise, get 10 businesses with no website in your city, free:

If you sell websites, you've heard it before. Three simple answers:

✔ The page talks to their followers. The website talks to people who don't know them yet.
