FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Download the app, double-click it, and it opens in your browser. Everything is done with the mouse, in English or French.

Do I need a Google key?

Yes: Démo Express reads listings with Google's official Places API and your own key. It takes a few minutes to create in Google Cloud (the guide is on the thank-you page) and requires a Google billing account. Google bills the calls at its rates, with a free monthly allowance; Démo Express adds no fees.

Why don't the files I send include the Google photos?

Google's rules forbid copying its photos, reviews, ratings and opening hours outside the app. The files sent to the prospect (PDF, images, web page) therefore replace them with "Your photos here" placeholders. To show the real photos and reviews, present the preview in a meeting or by screen sharing.

Can I publish the mockup as a real website?

No: it's a demo, labelled as such. Once the client signs, their real website is built with their own photos and text, using the tool of your choice.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. Démo Express runs on Windows 10 / 11 and on Mac, with one version for Apple silicon Macs (M1 to M4) and one for Intel Macs.

Windows or my Mac shows a warning the first time, is that normal?

Yes, for recent apps they don't know yet. On Windows, click "More info" then "Run anyway". On Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". You only do this once.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer. Google listings and photos are only kept in memory during the demo, never saved. Your settings and your Google key (encrypted) stay on your computer; only your licence key is checked online once a day.

How does the licence work?

Right after payment, your licence key is shown on the thank-you page; it works on 2 computers. Paste it at first launch, that's all. It is rechecked online once a day; without internet, the app stays usable for 14 days. To change computers, release the licence in one click. If refunded, the licence is deactivated.
